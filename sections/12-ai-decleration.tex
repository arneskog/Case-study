\section*{Declaration of AI Use}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Declaration of AI Use}
\guide{Required by the course: fill in NTNU's standard AI declaration form
(``Declaration of AI tools'', English version on innsida) and add the
statement to the submission. How to include it:
(1) fill in the form and save it as a PDF;
(2) upload it to the \texttt{figures} folder in Overleaf as
\texttt{ai-declaration.pdf};
(3) recompile: the form is added automatically after this page.
If the course also has a separate upload field for the form in Inspera, upload
it there too.}

\textbf{Statement:} \fillin{e.g. ``Group 106 used [tool and version] as
required by the assignment, to generate first drafts for Tasks 1--5 that were
evaluated and corrected by the group. All AI-generated text is labelled in
Appendix B and critiqued in Appendix A. The group has checked all facts and
sources and takes full responsibility for the content.''}
